## Purpose

Ivory KLEIO investigates the representation and processing of historical information. The intended kernel must preserve source attribution, contextual assumptions and distinctions between forms of uncertainty through operations and storage.

An agent harness is proposed as a scholarly interface to the kernel. It would support the formulation and inspection of interpretations and operations. The kernel must also be usable independently of an LLM.

## Scope of the specification

The specification addresses historical values, their interpretation, the rules governing operations, and the persistence of their meaning. The relationships between source tokens, interpretations, assertions and entities remain under investigation.

The current requirements are candidates. Adoption of a domain baseline depends on systematic analysis of the relevant Ivory and Kleio literature. The [source inventory](generated/sources/) records the consulted representations and their reading states.

## First experimental contract

The proposed Rust reference model distinguishes exhaustive temporal alternatives from an inclusive year interval.

| Interpretation of one event | Could the event have occurred in 1705? |
|---|---|
| Exactly one of the years 1700 and 1710 applies | No |
| Any year from 1700 through 1710 is possible | Yes |

The values are synthetic. The question concerns possibility under the recorded interpretation. No probability distribution is assigned.

The experiment must retain source wording and interpretation identifiers, return a structured explanation, and preserve the result through JSON save and reload. The [experimental contract](generated/specification/#minimum-prototype) defines its scope and error behavior.

## Implementation boundary

The website publishes the current documentation and worked examples. It does not execute historical database operations.

The Rust reference model, persistent engine and agent harness remain unimplemented. Historical calendar handling, general uncertainty semantics and physical storage design are open research and engineering questions.
