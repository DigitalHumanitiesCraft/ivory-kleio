# Plan

The [requirements integration](requirements-integration.md) connects the dissertation's existing results, the R representation needs and H operation candidates. The conceptual consolidation preserves stable identifiers and identifies specific unresolved meanings. The bounded Rust experiment remains an executable hypothesis within the existing undertaking.

## Work position

The dissertation-backed foundation and example locators are integrated. The theoretical repository maintains representation vocabulary, primitive hypotheses, comparison notes and synthetic teaching cases. Ivory KLEIO maintains operation candidates, executable contracts and the shared mapping. Repository boundaries do not establish independent subprojects.

[Testing](testing.md) supplies repeatable procedures. The [September journal entry](journal.md#2026-09-17-integrated-deterministic-testing-and-knowledge-refactor) records the previously observed Rust and website checks and their limits. The consolidation adds documentation evidence rather than a new historical validation or agent implementation.

## Open work

- With the expert, develop one existing dissertation example into a declared research operation. Establish its precise source location, transcription and reuse conditions, select the relevant R/H links, and state expected results and counterexamples independently of an implementation.
- Resolve the existing distinctions between simultaneous perspectives, exclusive alternatives and shared assumptions. Connect source form and normalization, and distinguish historical time roles from revision/decision time as the case requires.
- Evaluate the primitive reconstruction by its additional explanatory or practical contribution. A complete axiom system is not a prerequisite for the existing operational work.
- Review the temporal contract for scholarly adequacy within its synthetic scope before proposing a historical extension. General persistence, transactions, revision history and the agent case retain their own unimplemented requirements.
- Complete source-specific Ivory and Kleio reading where an attributed claim depends on it. The dissertation's chapter-level mapping is complete for the present scope; its bibliography has not been independently revalidated.
- Check a chosen comparison against equivalent information and operations before reporting a limitation or advantage. The theoretical repository's exploratory ratings remain unvalidated.
- Review website layout, navigation, search and keyboard use on narrow and wide screens after the content integration. Publication follows the website acceptance criteria and the owner's authorization.

## Research boundaries

The broad object model, uncertainty calculus, persistence architecture and harness runtime remain open. The new mapping organizes existing work and supplies no adopted domain baseline. Browser execution must agree with the Rust operation. A historical fixture, a controlled comparison and a scholar's acceptance require their own evidence.
